Every Outcome.
Clearly Accounted For.

There is no “pending at a price” state. A reference is an estimate until the cross.

01

Filled

The full eligible amount crosses. Your fill shows the stock, size, price, fee, and window.

02

Partly filled

The matched amount becomes a fill. The rest follows your wait-or-cancel policy.

03

Not filled

No eligible match or your limit prevented execution. The unfilled amount follows your policy.

04

Skipped

A halt or unreliable reference prevents the cross. No fill occurs; the selected policy still applies.

05

Cancelled

Before the cross, release the committed amount and make it available again.

06

Paused

No new orders are accepted. Existing records, balances, fills, and the tape remain readable.

A reference.
Not a promise.

The current reference price helps you estimate an order. It does not lock the price. The cross uses its external reference at execution, and your limit determines whether your order remains eligible.
