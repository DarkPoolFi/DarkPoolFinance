SHIELDED POOL · ROBINHOOD CHAIN

Notes, not accounts.

Your balance lives in notes inside the DarkPoolShieldedPool contract. Every proof is made in this browser; the operator seals and settles windows but never learns who owns what.

Your shielded account

LOCKED

Sign once to derive your private keys from your wallet. It sends no transaction and nothing about your keys is stored on a server. The same wallet recovers everything on any device.

Deposit

FROM YOUR WALLET

A deposit shows its wallet and amount on chain; what you do with it afterwards does not. Keep about 0.001 ETH extra in your wallet: proof transactions reserve their full gas up front, and unused gas is refunded.

Sealed order

THIS WINDOW

RFQ block trade

NEGOTIATED · SEALED

Agree a block with one counterparty. Requests and replies are sealed to each other's RFQ code, so the operator only relays ciphertext. When you both agree, each side seals an order into the same window; the pair crosses whole at the window reference, or not at all.

—

Share this code only with your counterparty. It is a session key, not your wallet, and can read RFQ messages but never spend funds.

Both orders are sealed automatically in the agreed window, through the relayer, so keep this tab open until then. Have a spare ETH note for the relayer fee ready first (Sealed order offers to prepare one), and for a sell, the tokens.

Notes

MERGE · SPLIT

Merge joins your two largest notes that come from the same deposit (notes from different deposits never combine). Split carves out a separate note, for example an ETH note to pay relayed orders from. Token notes are always submitted from your wallet.

Disclose to an auditor

SELECTIVE

Seals your viewing keys to the auditor and publishes them on chain. The auditor can then see every note, order and fill of this account, past and future, but can never spend. This cannot be undone.

Your shielded orders

PRIVATE TO YOU

Backstop liquidity

LIQUIDITY PROVIDERS

The backstop vault fills what is left of each window at the reference price plus or minus a published spread. Providers add ETH and a stock token to one market and share its gains and losses; withdrawals take the same share of both, at any time.

Moving from your private balance

MIGRATION

Funds in the previous private balance stay there until you move them; nothing is moved for you. While any remain, the Private balance tab shows them with a withdraw button: withdraw to your own wallet, then deposit the same funds here.
