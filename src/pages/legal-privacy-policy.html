About this preview

This notice describes this DarkpoolFi website and its interactive dashboard preview. Live trading has not launched. The preview does not connect to a wallet, accept deposits, or submit orders to a market.

Information in the dashboard

Asset searches, example funding amounts, and preview orders are processed in memory in your current browser page. This application does not send those entries to a trading service or store them in a user database. Reloading or leaving the page resets the preview.

Website delivery

The hosting platform may process technical request information, such as your IP address, browser details, request time, and requested page, to deliver and protect the website. Access to a private preview may require authentication managed by that platform. This website does not receive your sign-in password.

Cookies and external services

The DarkpoolFi application does not add advertising trackers, marketing cookies, or third-party analytics. Fonts and artwork are served with the website. The hosting provider may use its own necessary authentication or security mechanisms. The X and Telegram buttons open DarkpoolFi’s accounts on those services, which apply their own privacy terms.

Telegram bot

The @DarkPoolFi_bot Telegram bot answers questions with public venue data and does not store your messages. If you turn on a settlement ping for an order, it keeps your Telegram chat ID, the window number and your language until the ping is sent, then deletes them; /stop deletes them sooner. A price alert keeps your chat ID, the market, the direction, the price and your language until it fires or you remove it. It never receives your wallet, keys, orders, sides or sizes.

Your choices

You can use the preview without entering personal information. Do not enter real wallet secrets, payment details, or confidential trading information. Close or reload the page to clear the dashboard preview state. For hosting account information, use the privacy controls of your hosting account.

Before live launch

The operating entity, privacy contact, relevant jurisdictions, live data flows, retention periods, and rights-request process have not been supplied for this preview. A complete live-service privacy notice must identify those details before real account or transaction information is collected. This preview notice is not a statement of the future trading service’s data practices.
