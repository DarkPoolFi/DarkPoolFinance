04 / TRANSPARENCY

Private orders.
Open answers.

Privacy has a defined scope. Here is what the venue hides, what it publishes, and what you should understand before trading.

What We
Don't Hide

A venue that hides orders should be clear about everything around them.

See the window for yourself. ↗
Today the operator is trusted.

DarkpoolFi runs as a trusted netting venue. The operator runs the crossing engine and can see orders once a window is sealed. Balances are kept in a ledger backed by a public ETH reserve and a public stock-token vault; the live figures below show both against what is owed. The next phase settles windows inside a shielded pool with zero-knowledge proofs, which removes the operator from the trust set.

Deposits are not screened yet.

Deposit screening arrives with the shielded pool. It will be a funding control, not a claim of regulatory approval, licensing, or anonymity.

What is private, and how private.

ETH moves through a third-party private transfer service in split, randomly timed transfers, so your wallet has no direct on-chain link to the reserve. The service keeps a fee and holds the funds for a few minutes. Stock-token deposits and withdrawals go directly to and from the vault and are visible on chain. While few people use the venue, transfers are easier to link; the live statistics below show how many users they mix.

Public volume is delayed.

Total volume for each window is published after a delay. The public tape is always labeled delayed; it does not reveal orders waiting in the current window.

Withdrawals aren't gated.

Available funds can be withdrawn at any time. ETH is paid out through the same private transfer path, minus the transfer service fee; stock tokens leave the vault directly. Funds committed to an order are released by cancelling before the cross or through its result.

One price isn't a best-price promise.

Orders for the same stock cross at one outside reference price. The price is shared; it is not guaranteed to be the best available anywhere.

A window can be skipped.

If the reference is unreliable or an asset is halted, that asset's window may not cross. Nothing fills. The unfilled amount follows your selected policy.

Availability has limits.

Private trading of these assets is not available where restricted. Asset access and eligibility will be confirmed before live trading launches.

Trading is live, and small.

Connected wallets trade with real ETH and Robinhood Stock Tokens in five-minute windows at Chainlink reference prices. Without a connected wallet, the dashboard shows an interactive preview with example values. The crossing rules are published and every settlement is re-checked before balances move.
