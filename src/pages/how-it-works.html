02 / HOW IT WORKS

From private balance
to private result.

Four steps, with nothing left to guess. Funding, order entry, the cross, and withdrawal follow the same sequence in your dashboard.

01

Fund privately.

Start with USDG on Robinhood Chain. Review the amount, check the network, and move it into your private balance. Deposits are screened.

Available funds are separate from funds committed to an order.

FUND YOUR BALANCE ↗
02

Place a sealed order.

Select a stock, buy or sell, and enter a size. Add an optional price limit and choose whether any unfilled amount should wait or cancel.

Review the estimated value and fee before sealing your order.

BUILD AN ORDER ↗
03

The window crosses.

Orders collect until a fixed moment. At the cross, eligible orders for the same stock execute at the same outside reference price.

Results may be full, partial, unfilled, or skipped. The dashboard explains each outcome.

SEE YOUR ACTIVITY ↗
04

Leave whenever.

Withdraw available USDG when you choose. If funds are committed, cancel before the cross or wait for the order outcome.

The withdrawal flow separates available funds from active commitments.

EXPLORE WITHDRAWALS ↗

THE WAITING ROOM

Silence is part
of the design.

While your order waits, there is no public order book to watch and no queue position to improve. The countdown tells you when to return. Your order stays reachable, and you can cancel before crossing begins.
